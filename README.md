One image that sums up the world news of **Sunday 22 November 2026**, drawn as a
newspaper front page: COP31 climate talks ending with no deal in Antalya, tankers
struck near the Strait of Hormuz, a Russian drone wrecking flats near Kyiv, a
Russian warship hit at Benghazi, the Trump–Xi–Putin summit in Shenzhen, a global
AI stock sell-off, US Democrats winning 258 House seats, and BepiColombo entering
orbit around Mercury — plus a ticker of the day's other stories.

The repository holds the finished `entry.png` (1024×1024, drawn with Pillow by
`make_image.py` from that day's reported headlines) and a small standard-library
program, `main.py`, that copies it to `/output/entry.png` in the grader's
sandbox, where no network is available.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
